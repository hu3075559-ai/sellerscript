Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026. This is the short, honest version — it matches what the site actually does.

The tools collect nothing

All four SellerScript tools (script generator, title optimizer, hashtag generator, banned-words checker) run entirely in your browser against template and rule libraries built into the page. The product names, prices, titles and copy you type or paste are never uploaded, stored or sent to any server — ours or anyone else's.

The waitlist and contact form collect an email address

If you join the SellerScript Pro waitlist, we collect the email address you enter. If you send us a message through the contact form on the About page, we collect your email address and the message you write. Submissions are sent to and stored in our lead-capture database. That is the only personal data this site collects.

How we use it

Waitlist addresses are used for exactly one thing: a single email when SellerScript Pro launches. Contact-form messages are used to read and reply to your message. We do not sell, rent or share email addresses with third parties for marketing, and we do not send newsletters or promotional mail to the waitlist.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to see aggregate usage — which tools get used, roughly from which countries — so we know what to improve. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes usage data under Google's privacy policy. It is not linked to your waitlist email address.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your email address and any message you sent at any time. Use the contact form on the About page (from the same address, so we can find the record) and we will remove it from the database. If Pro has not launched yet, deleting your address simply takes you off the waitlist.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters — for example, if Pro accounts launch and start storing saved templates — we will update this page and note the date before the change takes effect.
