FAQ

    
How long should a TikTok Shop sales script be?
At ~150 spoken words per minute, 15 seconds needs about 35–45 words and 30 seconds about 75–90. Every generated script shows its word count and estimated speaking time.

    
What structure do converting videos use?
Hook in the first 3 seconds, the buyer's pain, a real demo, then the offer with a clear call to action. That is exactly the structure this generator follows.

    
Can I get a different version?
Yes — "New variation" recombines hooks, pain lines, demos and CTAs from the template library. Generate a few takes and film the one that fits you.
