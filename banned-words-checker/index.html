FAQ

Which words get listings in trouble?

Unverifiable superlatives (best, cheapest, #1), medical claims for ordinary products (cure, heal, lose 10 lbs), fake urgency (only today, selling out fast), income promises, and proof claims you cannot back up (clinically proven, officially approved).

Does a clean scan mean I am compliant?

No. Policies vary by country and category and change over time, and review also covers images and video. Treat this as a first pass and check the TikTok Shop policy centre for your category.

Is my text uploaded?

No. The scan runs fully in your browser against a built-in rule library.
