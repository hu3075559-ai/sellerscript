About SellerScript

What this is

SellerScript is a small, independent tool site for TikTok Shop sellers. It offers four free tools — a video script generator, a product title optimizer, a hashtag generator and a banned-words checker — built to cover the repetitive copy work around listing and posting: the script before you film, the title before you publish, the tags and the risk scan before you post.

Who runs it

The site is built and maintained by the SellerScript team, an independent tools project. It is not a TikTok product and is not affiliated with TikTok or TikTok Shop in any way. A Pro tier (saved templates, batch tools, deeper policy scans) is in preparation — the waitlist on any page is how we will announce it.

How the tools work

There is no AI behind the tools, and nothing you type is uploaded. Each tool is a template or rule engine that runs entirely in your browser: the script generator assembles proven selling structures (hook → pain → demo → offer) around your product facts; the banned-words checker matches your copy against a built-in library of risky-claim patterns drawn from public marketplace advertising policy. That design is deliberate — results are instant, free to run, and your draft listings stay on your device. It also means outputs are starting points, not guarantees: always check the current TikTok Shop policy centre for your country and category before publishing.
